# Mag-Check-Hud---Anomaly
Mag Check HUD An add-on for Mags Redux (STALKER Anomaly)  Mag Check HUD gives you a quick, readable ammo count whenever you do the Mags Redux mag check. Instead of the vague “About Half” message, you see the actual numbers, like 16/30 5.56x45 AP, in the bottom-right of your screen. It goes away as soon as your weapon is back in your hands.
